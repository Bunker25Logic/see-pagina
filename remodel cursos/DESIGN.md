## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, institutional, yet technologically refined public portal for civic and educational management under SEE-AC (Secretaria de Estado de Educação do Acre). Built with the `Safe` visual identity, it fuses governmental clarity and universal accessibility with crisp tabular hierarchy, streamlined digital services, and civic transparency.

The visual ethos blends **Corporate / Modern** institutional rigor with subtle **Glassmorphic** accents for navigational and elevated floating elements. The interface projects reliability, administrative efficiency, and direct accessibility for students, families, educators, and civil servants. High-contrast statistical metrics, clean line boundaries, and clear communication channels (WhatsApp direct links, administrative coordination contacts) transform conventional bureaucratic information into an empowering, approachable civic utility.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic civic identity representing Acre's environmental heritage and educational vitality:
- **Primary (`#059669`)**: Vibrant civic emerald green used for primary interactive states, key CTA buttons, active tabs, and validation marks.
- **Secondary (`#0f172a`)**: Deep authority slate blue used for structural hierarchy, high-contrast headings, institutional footer backgrounds, and primary data labels.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706`)**: Warm amber gold utilized for alerts, enrollment deadlines, vocational badges, and official priority indicators.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Balanced slate gray calibrating secondary typography, icon outlines, metadata tags, and non-active state borders.

Surface colors emphasize clean separation: the main canvas sits on crisp `#f8fafc`, container cards utilize pure `#ffffff`, and borders rely on precise `#e2e8f0` dividers to preserve legibility without visual noise. Semantic education tracks (Fundamental, Médio, Integral, Profissionalizante) carry standardized low-saturation container tints to ensure instant visual categorization.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is executed entirely in **Inter**, ensuring optimal legibility across desktop screens and lower-end mobile devices common in regional access scenarios.

- **Numerics & Institutional Statistics**: Census data, enrollment counts, and performance metrics leverage tabular lining numerals (`tnum`) in heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tight letter spacing (`-0.03em`) to anchor dashboards with executive authority.
- **Headings**: Institutional titles use semibold and bold weights with subtle negative tracking, creating a modern, editorial presentation that commands respect without aggressive visual brutality.
- **Micro-labels & Badges**: Educational tracks and status indicators utilize small caps and uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) to ensure legibility when badges appear within dense tabular cards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column fluid grid** system on desktop and a **4-column responsive grid** on mobile, constrained to a maximum content width of `1280px` (`80rem`) centered on the canvas.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) side margins. Institutional school cards utilize a 2-column layout (6 columns each) or 3-column layout (4 columns each) depending on information density.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.5rem` (`24px`) side margins. Institutional cards reflow into a clean 2-column or single-column layout.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` (`16px`) margins. Cards span the full width, and secondary contact buttons stack into accessible touch targets (minimum `44px` height).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **tonal layering and crisp low-contrast contours** supplemented by soft ambient micro-shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Neutral surface `#f8fafc` serves as the non-elevated ground layer.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier)**: `#ffffff` containers with a `1px` structural outline in `#e2e8f0` and an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Quick Stats)**: On cursor focus and hover states, cards smoothly elevate with `transform: translateY(-2px)` accompanied by `box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a colored border transition toward `primary` tint.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars, Modals & Floating Badges)**: Translucent surface layers utilizing `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a delicate border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

In alignment with roundedness level `1` (Soft), elements follow a restrained institutional geometry:
- Standard elements (inputs, sub-containers, internal contact bars): `0.25rem` (`4px`) to `0.375rem` (`6px`).
- Medium containers and institutional cards: `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`).
- Modals, prominent hero containers, and stat callouts: `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`).
- Pills and interactive category filters: fully rounded pill shapes are reserved exclusively for contextual tags, status pills, and toggle chips to indicate interactive statefulness without softening the formal architecture of the portal.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Institutional Button**: Solid `#059669` background with white text, `8px` corner radius, `44px` minimum height, subtle inner highlight, and smooth transition to `#047857` on hover.
- **Direct WhatsApp Channel Button**: Distinctive `#25d366` green surface with bold white text and official chat glyph, providing high contrast and unmistakable utility for immediate community contact.
- **Secondary / Utility Button**: Transparent background with `1px` solid `#cbd5e1` border, slate `#0f172a` text, and `#f1f5f9` surface on hover.

### 2. Institutional School Cards
- Structured layout featuring a standardized top header: School Acronym Avatar (e.g., "CF", "KJ") on the left with level indicators (e.g., "ENSINO FUNDAMENTAL", "ENSINO MÉDIO") and total active enrollment count on the right.
- Distinct title typography with address and localized district tag.
- Internal administrative sub-cards: clean compartmentalized boxes (`#f8fafc` background with `#e2e8f0` micro-borders) highlighting **Direção Escolar** and **Coordenação de Ensino** with direct telephone copy icons and WhatsApp launch triggers.
- Bottom action rail featuring secondary direct phone links and prominent interactive communication triggers.

### 3. Filters & Search Pill Bar
- Segmented filter bar grouping: "Todas as Instituições", "Fundamental & Médio", "Ensino Fundamental", and "Cursos Gratuitos".
- Active pills receive a solid dark slate `#0f172a` or primary emerald `#059669` fill with crisp white text. Inactive pills utilize `#ffffff` with `#e2e8f0` borders and `#64748b` typography.
- Search input is integrated with inline search icon, instant clear trigger, and alphabetical sorting dropdown menu (`Ordem Alfabética A-Z`).

### 4. Stat & Metric Counters
- Metric cards (Polos de Ensino, Matrículas Ativas, Comunicação Direta) feature high-contrast oversized numerals (`2rem`), crisp auxiliary status icons wrapped in circular pastel emerald containers, and live operational status indicators.

### 5. Official State Footer
- Deep institutional navy/slate `#0f172a` surface with formal SEE-AC branding, government seal, physical office address (`Núcleo de Educação de Brasiléia`), operational hours, interactive localized mini-map widget, and accessibility transparency links.
